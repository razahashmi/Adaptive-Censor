# Community conduct

Be respectful and specific. Critique ideas and evidence, not people. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized comments about contributors, exposure of private information and repeated disruption are not acceptable.

Content-filter preferences differ. Discuss categories, annotations and errors without making personal judgments about viewers or performers. Post only permitted examples and use concise content notices where needed.

Maintainers may edit/remove harmful material, close disruptive discussions or restrict participation. Explain moderation decisions when practical. Report concerns privately through the repository's private reporting channel when available; otherwise ask a maintainer for a private contact without posting sensitive details. Do not send reports to an invented address.

These expectations apply to project issues, reviews, discussions and other project spaces.
