# Support

Start with [installation](docs/INSTALLATION.md), the player's **Check setup**, [limitations](LIMITATIONS.md) and the [model card](docs/MODEL_CARD.md).

Use [Issues](https://github.com/razahashmi/Adaptive-Censor/issues) for reproducible bugs, installation results and feature proposals. The forms separate player bugs, installation, detection quality and research changes. No response time or supported household deployment is promised for this preview.

Filtering reports in the player stay local. They are not automatically sent to GitHub or to maintainers. Do not post copied media, transcripts, raw reports, private paths or tokens. For vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
